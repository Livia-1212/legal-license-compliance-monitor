# Attorney License Compliance Monitor

## Project Overview

Attorney License Compliance Monitor is a compliance management platform designed to help law firms and legal departments monitor attorney licensing, Continuing Legal Education (CLE) requirements, jurisdiction-specific eligibility, and matter assignments from a centralized system.

The project demonstrates how legal and regulatory requirements can be translated into structured data, compliance workflows, and operational controls.

Rather than serving as a document repository, the platform is designed to support compliance monitoring, administrative efficiency, and risk reduction throughout the attorney licensing lifecycle.

---

# Business Problem

Many law firms continue to manage attorney licenses, CLE obligations, renewal deadlines, and jurisdiction-specific practice requirements across multiple spreadsheets, emails, and manual tracking processes.

As organizations grow, these disconnected processes increase the risk of:

- Missed license renewal deadlines
- Incomplete CLE records
- Incorrect matter assignments
- Inconsistent jurisdiction eligibility checks
- Limited audit visibility
- Administrative inefficiency

The project explores how these operational risks can be reduced through centralized compliance management.

---

# Intended Users

This platform is designed for:

- Law Firm Administrators
- Legal Operations Professionals
- Compliance Teams
- Attorneys licensed in multiple jurisdictions
- Professional Responsibility / Risk Management teams

---

# Solution

The Attorney License Compliance Monitor centralizes attorney licensing information, CLE compliance, jurisdiction rules, and matter assignments into a single operational dashboard.

The platform enables organizations to:

- Maintain attorney licensing records
- Track CLE completion status
- Monitor license expiration dates
- Evaluate jurisdiction-specific eligibility
- Support matter assignment decisions
- Improve compliance visibility
- Reduce manual administrative work

# Current Status

This project is currently a functional prototype demonstrating compliance workflow design and legal operations management concepts.
